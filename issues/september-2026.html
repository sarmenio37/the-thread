Sarah
📍 Cleveland, Ohio
⚽
✨ Highlight: Survived a chaotic month of rollercoasters, rusted bolts, and raucous nights — but hey, I replaced my Jeep shocks!

I’m writing this slightly inebriated on a flight back from Baltimore after a spontaneous trip to soothe an irate customer. This month has been a bit of a rollercoaster, but a mostly good one! I only shed tears once, thanks to a missed flight and a few major production issues. But I finally feel like I'm in a position to really instigate some change at work. It's empowering and, dare I say, exciting to see a path forward and have the authority to walk it.

On the home front, Vela is growing like a weed, and it's the little moments in the morning and at breakfast that make my heart sing. We took her to Cedar Point, where she declared the first ride 'too slow' before diving headfirst into rollercoasters and making fast friends with a girl named Veronica. It was adorable chaos. Meanwhile, Chris is getting busy with ski patrol and has whipped up an e-commerce system for the ski swap. I even tackled replacing the shocks on my Jeep, rusted bolts and all, because why not add 'amateur mechanic' to my list of skills?

Family-wise, my sister had a scare with some emergency GI issues but is thankfully on the mend. We enjoyed an OSU game with my niece and nephew, and I might have introduced my niece to Rumple Minze — oops! Vela got some cousin time with Sebby at the zoo, which was a blast. We’re gearing up for a Modest Mouse concert, hoping for a good date night despite the city noise keeping Chris up at night. We're dreaming of quieter days come winter.

As for my creative endeavors, the memoir is moving at a snail's pace, but I'm getting there. I attended WITCON with Marissa, blissfully free from planning duties this year. We also had some peaceful moments on the boat watching the airshow. Life is a mix of chaos and calm, and I’m here for it.
