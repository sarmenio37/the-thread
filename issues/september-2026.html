✨ Highlight: Chaperoned a pumpkin patch trip and attended a surprise 40th — life's full of surprises!

Life's funny, isn't it? One moment you're chugging 4 Lokos while watching Jersey Shore with your college roommates, and the next, you're chaperoning 50 kindergarteners on their inaugural school field trip to a pumpkin patch. Fifteen years can really sneak up on you like that.

We've finally got a deck with furniture, but of course, Mother Nature has decided to keep us indoors. She's quite the diva, isn't she?

I crashed a surprise 40th birthday bash for Sharissa, the one and only! Our five-year-old daughters became besties, which was adorable to witness. I knocked back a green tea shot with the birthday girl at 4 PM. Woof. Her family is absolutely hilarious, and who knew she'd end up coaching kids' soccer? Life's full of surprises!

It's definitely fall again, and you can tell by the mussel surveys, Buckeyes football, and the slow, tragic demise of the mums on my porch. Autumn, you relentless rascal!

Flying back from a spontaneous trip to Baltimore with a slightly inebriated grin—thank you, grumpy customer—I can finally say it's been a decent month. I only shed a tear when I missed my NYC flight amidst three production down issues but hey, I'm at the point where I can see the change that's needed and actually make it happen. On the personal front, Vela is growing like a weed. We've shared some fantastic moments—she had a blast at Cedar Point, terrifying herself on the rollercoasters and making a new friend, Veronica. Vela's started soccer practice and is acing it, alongside her Little Gym and swimming lessons. My sister had an emergency surgery, but she's okay now. Chris is diving back into ski patrol, and he even built an e-commerce system for them.

We caught an OSU game with my niece and nephew, where I may have introduced my niece to the wonders of Rumple Minze—whoops!—and discovered a new favorite spot, the Ruckmore Bar. Sebby joined us for a zoo adventure, giving Vela some quality cousin time. In a personal triumph, I tackled the rusted bolts on my Jeep's shocks, mostly on my own, saving a fortune. Chris is almost done with the front porch, and we’re off to see Modest Mouse tomorrow for a much-needed date night.

Life's little quirks continue: birthday parties with parents you barely know—always an adventure. Kira, our shedding dog, dreams puppy dreams, and Chris battles insomnia from the bass thumping through the walls from the Flats’ bars. City life, huh? We're eyeing a move, but with interest rates doing their dance, we're waiting for spring to see what the market does. We did manage a boat trip to watch the airshow—it was neat to share that with family and friends. The memoir's still a slow burn, but progress is progress. Attending WITCON with Marissa was a treat—no planning required this year!

A note from all three of us

As we navigate the chaos of parenting, surprise parties, and spontaneous adventures, it seems we've all found ourselves in unexpected moments of joy and community—from Mac's crawling attempts to Vela's rollercoaster camaraderie and deckside giggles with green tea shots. Autumn may be nipping at our heels and life might have us dancing to its unpredictable rhythm, but we've got each other's stories to keep us grounded and grinning. Here's to finding our tribe and the laughter that comes with it, even when Mother Nature insists we stay indoors.
