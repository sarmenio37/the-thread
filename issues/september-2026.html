We attended a birthday party for Vela’s friend Harper, and as always, birthday parties remain a circus of unknown parents and their offspring. Kira, our shedding but still wonderful pup, continues to have adorable puppy dreams.

Chris isn’t sleeping well thanks to the bass thumping from the nearby bars, so we’re contemplating a move. But with interest rates doing the cha-cha, we might wait until spring. Earlier this month, we got to enjoy an airshow from the boat, which was a neat experience. Still chugging along on my memoir, though it’s a slow process. Attended WITCON with Marissa, which was blissfully stress-free this year since all I had to do was attend!
