Between mussel surveys, Buckeyes football, and unintentionally murdering innocent mums on my porch, I’m pretty sure it's officially fall, y’all!

As for the rest, Kira is still my loyal companion, shedding away and dreaming her puppy dreams. Chris is battling sleepless nights due to the bass from nearby bars. City life, right? We’re considering a move, but with interest rates as they are, we might wait until spring. Oh, and I introduced my niece to Rumpleminze at the OSU game — an aunt's duty, right? Looking forward to the quieter fall and winter months.
