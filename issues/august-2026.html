This Month's Updates
Caroline
📍 Bend, Oregon
🏔️
✨ Highlight: Survived a double trip month with toddler antics, postpartum anxiety, and finally some moments of true relaxation!

August was a whirlwind, as you both probably guessed. Two trips in a month with only a week to catch my breath in between — what could possibly go wrong? Oh, just my postpartum anxiety deciding to pop in right before all the fun began. Made packing for the boys' appointments at Doernbecher Children's Hospital in Portland followed immediately by our beach trip an absolute circus. But hey, no full-blown panic attacks, so there's my humble brag! Seriously though, postpartum has been way more challenging this time, and Randy and I are working on our communication skills. We're totally fine, but it doesn't always feel like it because we're in the thick of it, and, well, postpartum anxiety!

Both the Ohio and Oregon coast trips were overall great, but note to Airbnb: please add a toddler-proof filter. The constant vigilance required with Remi this month was exhausting! However, he's at such an amazing stage — so curious and busy. He just added four new words: baby, no, all done, and ball. Perfect timing for preschool next week to see how far he can fly!

Mac is officially on the move with his scooting, circling, and barrel rolling. The kid's ready to explore and hates being alone but loves floor time with Big Brother Remi, albeit under heavy surveillance. 😂 I'm looking forward to the structure Remi's school will bring, which will hopefully help Mac and me get out more since car rides are his personal hell. A hint of a nap schedule might make adventures (or grocery shopping) less of a wailing fest.

Highlights this month include seeing my whole family, especially my dad, who was over the moon to meet Mac and Jordi. Remi managed to learn not to eat sand, which made our beach time delightful. Mac's belly laughs are now part of his repertoire, and they are heart-melting. And finally, Randy and I are starting to feel like the team I know we are. Our Ohio trip, while stressful, helped us work through some bumps, making our Coast trip way more enjoyable. We even found moments of genuine relaxation!

Oh, and I just finished book eight in the Wheel of Time series — only six more to go! Meanwhile, Randy's breezed through all 14, because of course, he has. But honestly, the books are way better than the Amazon Prime series.

Kyla
📍 Columbus, Ohio
🌷
✨ Highlight: Survived the summer chaos and started the deck project — finally!

Adjusting to two kids needing to be in two different places at the same time—it's like a logistical puzzle that no one prepared me for. And somehow, Irie seems to have aged five years in the past week alone. Time really does fly when you're not paying attention.

After what felt like an eternal battle of permits and approvals, our deck project is finally underway. I can almost taste the morning coffees on it already. Meanwhile, Mavery has decided that walking is her new full-time gig. She’s on a mission to trip on every piece of concrete and climb every piece of furniture like it's Everest. It's a 'break her face' movement over here, and I'm just trying to keep up.

In an attempt to bring some order into our chaotic lives, we're finally getting our laundry detergent off the floor and trying to put cabinets up in the laundry room. Baby steps, right? The girls are set to be future flower girls in my niece's wedding this October. Expect cuteness overload.

I'm sulking because summer is ending, and I missed seeing you guys in real life. Here’s to hoping for a real-life visit soon—fingers crossed that it happens within the next five years.
