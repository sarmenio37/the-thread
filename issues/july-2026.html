✨ Highlight: Surviving life with two under two and gearing up for preschool chaos!

July... or as I like to call it, "What year is it again?" Life with two under two has me feeling like I'm stuck in a time warp. I dream of having hobbies again and being interesting to non-parents — maybe in five years? The reality of it is both beautiful and exhausting, with moments that make me feel like either a superstar mom or a complete disaster, but 85% of the time, we're leaning toward superstar.

Remi had his preschool visit and absolutely crushed it! He sat through circle time like a champ (we'll see how long that lasts) and only threw one toy that actually hit someone. His occupational therapist is helping us modify a chair so he can sit with everyone at lunch like a big kid—I'm so impressed with the Montessori staff and their readiness to adapt to his needs. Meanwhile, he's tearing around the house with more energy than a pack of caffeinated squirrels, spreading mittens everywhere like he's preparing for winter.

Mac, our little rolling prodigy, is already doing barrel rolls off his play mat, and Randy's mom cheerfully reminded us that Randy started crawling at five months. We're bracing ourselves for the chaos! But hey, Mac is finally sleeping in his bassinet, which is a game-changer for my sleep quality, even if I'm writing this at 2:15 AM. He's a smiley, social little guy, and I love it.

Randy's about to take some pseudo-leave, which hopefully means I'll have some backup. We managed without his mom's help during his last work trip, thanks to our old nanny, and I think I could handle up to three nights on my own now. As for Randy, finding ways for him to de-stress is a work in progress—he's not quite ready to tackle those "obvious" stress levels. I'm finally feeling strength return to my postpartum body after major surgery, and I'm dreaming of regular workouts once Remi starts school. I've decided to go back to work on November 1st, sticking to 20 hours a week since I didn't even get an interview for that full-time job (ha!). I can always quit if it doesn't work out! Can't wait to see you guys in exotic Cincinnati, Ohio, and I promise to have more diverse updates beyond family life soon!

✨ Highlight: Survived a Disney princess party and rediscovered my groove on a mountain bike!

Thoroughly enjoyed dancing our faces off at a super kid-friendly 4th of July cousin wedding. Trying to survive Brandon’s newly rekindled sports card collecting hobby. Had a parents' night out with friends to see Hardy and Posty… it was weird but fun. And a manageable hangover was the icing on the cake.

Irie came to work with me for a day and made s'mores, went on a treasure hunt, and met a K-9 officer (and my boring coworkers). Successfully threw a Disney princess 5th birthday party for Irie with a guest appearance by Queen Elsa. That was real awkward. Definitely thought I made a huge mistake when none of the kids would respond to her with anything other than a blank stare (never meet your heroes?!) but luckily everyone warmed up to her and the 'Let It Go' live performance will be a pastel happy memory… thank god.

Harvested my first veggies from my garden! So damn rewarding! More teeth for poor Mavery. But in the rare moments where she doesn’t have her pacifier in, she’s trying to talk (mostly 'dad, dada, daddy' and anything she can put in her mouth is 'baba' but we get the occasional 'mama' which melts me). She’s taking steps, up to 8! But still prefers to crawl, cruise, or have us hold her hand… I’m not mad about it :)

And since it took months of emotional re-regulation for the last big change, we’re starting to mentally prepare for Irie to start kindergarten in 4 weeks. Sadly, no one from her pre-k class will be at her new school :( I think it might be pretty rough on her but maybe not (hopefully). Teleporting back to 2009 with Linds Mo tomorrow night to see Lil Wayne and 2 Chaaaaiiiinnnnzzzzz. So stoked. Just clocked my first mountain bike ride in almost 2 years. Definitely short and slow but damn it felt gooooooodddd anyway.

Love and miss you guys, excited for our next catch up to be IN PERSON!!!!!!!!!!!!!!

Sarah
📍 Cleveland, Ohio
⚽
✨ Highlight: Survived July's chaos, celebrated Gavin's graduation, and scored my dream job — all while mastering the art of the impromptu cookout.

Holy smokes, where did July go? I was supposed to take it easier this month, but life had other plans. I kicked off July with a supposed week off, but my boss’s boss had me dive into an executive project to secure a strategic development partnership with NYU Langone. Naturally, that ate up my downtime. To add a cherry on top, one of my team members was out at a customer site when his product decided to go haywire. So, there I was, playing superhero with my dev team manager, fixing what could be fixed and promising future solutions for what couldn't. It felt like everything was blowing up at once, and work was, in a word, relentless.

Despite the chaos, there were some bright spots. I took my daughter Vela to visit Kyla and Irie, and we hit the water park for a splashy escape from the heat dome — we’re talking 95 degrees and up. Chris had planned to fix our front porch during his week off, but the heat and a few pesky delays kept us sweating it out a bit longer. We also celebrated Chris’s birthday with an impromptu cookout relocation to his parents’ place after a power outage — just another day in the life, right?

And then, a glimmer of hope: Gavin graduated, and we celebrated his leap into college life. It feels like just yesterday I was changing his diapers! We also introduced Vela to the wonders of Toy Story 5. The initial theater noise had her worried, but once Woody and Buzz were on screen, she was hooked. Work finally calmed down by the end of the month. My new boss announced some exciting changes, and I'm thrilled to lead the roadmap for healthcare systems — with all the folks I want and none of the ones I don't. It’s like my dream job has finally landed in my lap. Plus, our tricky customers are going live and thriving! So, while July started with a bang (and not the fun kind), it’s ending on a high note. Can’t wait to see everyone in August!

A note from all three of us

As we wrap up July, it seems we're all juggling a beautiful chaos—whether it's preschool tours, veggie gardens, or surprise executive projects. From caffeinated-squirrel energy levels to unexpected cookout relocations, we've mastered the art of embracing the unpredictable. Here's to superstar moments, manageable hangovers, and the shared anticipation of a much-needed in-person reunion in exotic Cincinnati!
